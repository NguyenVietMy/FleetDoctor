# Outage and replay handling

Task03 implements outage and replay handling locally. AWS fault injection, deployment, rendered Grafana inspection and publication remain unrun. See [evidence status](task03-evidence.md) for the exact source acceptance and local proof records.

Collection uses scheduled monotonic five-second ticks independently of one uploader and one request. The spawned collection worker retains its one-second budget; the event loop awaits one worker call in a thread and joins that call before closing the worker on cancellation. Missed ticks are skipped. Uploads have a two-second whole-operation deadline, no redirects or environment proxies, one connection and two-second keepalive expiry. `--once` collects and makes one bounded attempt.

The process-local queue retains at most 60 batches and 2,000,000 exact serialized UTF-8 request-body bytes, including an in-flight batch. Each body is at most 65,536 bytes. Bytes, observation time, identity, metrics and delivery snapshot are encoded once. Oldest non-in-flight entries are evicted to fit; a pinned batch can cause the incoming batch to be discarded. Observations older than ten seconds are pruned; the newest remaining batch is selected. An in-flight entry stays pinned until completion, which removes that exact entry. Accepted/duplicate acknowledgements discard older backlog as superseded. No persistent spool or collector-restart dedup guarantee is claimed; Task04 owns those guarantees.

Transport errors, timeouts, HTTP408/429/5xx and malformed success responses retry after 1, 2, 4, then 5 seconds. New collections do not shorten that deadline. Terminal/success responses reset backoff. Other 4xx responses are terminal, including schema422 and identity409. A bounded JSON acknowledgement must contain a known status (`accepted`, `duplicate`, `superseded`, `ineligible`), `durable: false`, and `incidents_durable: true`. An optional identity must match all enclosing identity fields. Task02 collector acknowledgements without identity remain supported. Unknown status, HTML, empty or oversized responses never count as acceptance.

Deploy the new collector before new agents. Optional strict version1 `delivery: {"discard_total": N}` preserves acceptance of Task02 agents. Old agents have reporting unavailable and no fabricated zero-valued discard series. New agents against old collectors get terminal422 rather than an endless retry.

The discard total is a bounded, monotonic local count within an agent session. It counts deliberate removal without an accepted/duplicate acknowledgement (expiry, capacity, oversize, supersession, ineligible or permanent rejection). It cannot establish exact network loss: an acknowledgement may have been lost after acceptance. Pruning happens before the immutable snapshot; evictions caused by insertion appear in the next sample. Totals reset with a new session, and reports are eventually observable in later eligible samples.

`/nodes` and node metrics expose delivery availability, `scope: agent_session`, last observed discard total, observation age and freshness. Prometheus exports node-only labels on `fleetdoctor_delivery_reporting_available` and `fleetdoctor_delivery_discard_total`; the latter is a gauge and can decrease on a newer session. Both describe last observed reporting, so consult sample age/stale even when a discard gauge remains visible during the outage. Resource gauges disappear after ten seconds; stale becomes true after fifteen. Dashboard time series disable null connection and do not map missing reporting to zero or use last-value fill. History comes from live exports and never receives replay backfill.

Eligible observations use one captured collector time and inclusive age[-2,10] seconds. Only successfully persisted observations publish latest telemetry, delivery reporting, session retirement, fingerprints and evaluator state. The collector retains 60 accepted identity fingerprints per node and eight retired sessions; this is bounded process-local memory. Conflicting retained identities return409; older forgotten observations remain blocked by the latest observation time. Identical unpublished retries after503 still reach canonical incident reconciliation. Lost-COMMIT acknowledgement regression coverage remains required. Increasing sequence with nonincreasing time is superseded. Duplicate, rejected, superseded and ineligible samples cannot advance disk windows. Session changes, gaps over ten seconds and missing block data reset the affected windows. An active incident keeps its UUID, opening evidence, severity and configuration across a gap; three new eligible healthy samples resolve it.
